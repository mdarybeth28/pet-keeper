Pet Keeper — Privacy Policy

About Pet Keeper

Pet Keeper is an app and website that helps pet owners keep every pet's records in one place — shots, vet visits, medicines, and due dates — built by Mary Davis of Mary Beth's Finds in Tennessee. She first made it for her daughter, who was keeping track of five pets on paper and sticky notes.

Privacy, in plain words

What Pet Keeper stores: your account email and a password (the password is stored scrambled — nobody, including Mary, can read it), and the pet records you put in: pet names, kinds and breeds, birthdays, vet visits, shots, medicines, weights, due dates, and the photos you add — pet photos, and photos of records or receipts you send in to be read.

Reading photos: if you use the photo-reading help, the photo is sent to an AI reading service that pulls the words and numbers off it. What it read is always shown to you first, and nothing is saved until you say so.

What it never stores: your payment card details — subscriptions are handled by Stripe on the web, or by Apple inside the app, and they do the card part themselves. Pet Keeper also never sells your information, never shares it with advertisers, and never tracks you across other websites or apps.

Who can see your pets: only your account. Every account is walled off from every other account — signing in shows your pets and nobody else's.

Where it lives: Pet Keeper runs on GitHub Pages with its database at Supabase, an established U.S. database provider. Your records sit behind your password-protected login.

Delete your account or your data

Pet Keeper is made by Mary Beth's Finds. You can ask for your account, or just your data, to be deleted at any time:

How to ask: email affiliatefindstools@gmail.com from the email address on your Pet Keeper account, and say "Delete my account" (or "Delete my data"). We delete it within 30 days — usually much sooner — and email you back to confirm.

What gets deleted: your account and login email, all of your pet records, and any photos you added.

What is kept: only your deletion-request email itself, kept as the record that you asked. Nothing else. (If you have a paid subscription, deleting your account does not by itself cancel billing — cancel the subscription in the same place you bought it, Stripe or your Apple subscriptions, so you are not charged again.)

Keep the account, delete just the data: that works too — same email. Ask for your data to be deleted and your empty account stays open.
