Delete my account

If you want to leave Pet Keeper for good, you can delete your account right here. Mary does not have to do it for you.

This deletes your pets, records and account for good. It can't be undone.

One important thing: deleting your account does not by itself cancel a paid subscription. To stop being charged, cancel the subscription too — open the receipt Stripe emailed you when you joined and tap the cancel / manage link in it, or tap 💬 Tell Mary above and Mary will help you cancel. If you delete the account first, keep that receipt email — it is the easiest way to reach the cancel page afterwards.
